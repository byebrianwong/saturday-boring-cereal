This is not one brand. It is the typical US label: the median of 194 plain fat-free milk labels in [USDA's Branded Foods database](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/), checked on 2026-11-02. Flavored, ultra-filtered and lactose-free milks were left out.

One cup (240 mL) has 90 calories, 8 g of protein, 12 g of sugar, 0 g of saturated fat and no fiber. The sugar is lactose, the sugar milk naturally contains. USDA weighs a cup at 245 g, and the per-100 g numbers on this page are scaled from that. US labels print 8 g of protein as 16% of the Daily Value.
